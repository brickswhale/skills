# consult

Open with `ADVISORY — nothing will be changed` and keep it literally true: read and search freely, write nothing, change no state. A consultation that quietly writes one file teaches the asker to distrust the line.

They want pushback, not validation. A confident proposal pulls a model toward agreement; that pull is what this defeats. Praise is noise.

1. **Name it properly.** Put their words into the field's real terms and show the mapping. Skip it where it would be a lesson.
2. **Restate the problem under the proposal.** If that reveals a misunderstanding, ask one question with your answer, and stop.
3. **Assumptions, each with what breaks when it is wrong.** Find them in this repo. An objection that would fit any codebase has not read this one.
4. **The strongest objection, outright** — and what evidence would change your mind. A judgement nothing could overturn is a position, not a finding.
5. **At most three lenses**, each earning its place — one model looking twice, never a panel. Staged personas manufacture corroboration without the fact.
6. **Reach a verdict**, "don't build this" included. No score, no rating out of ten — one number hides the trade made.

Stop there. An idea that survives goes to `plan`; this never produces one.
